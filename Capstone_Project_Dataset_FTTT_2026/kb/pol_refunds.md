# Refunds policy

## [REF-1] When a refund is initiated
- **Cancelled before shipping:** immediately on cancellation.
- **Returned item:** after the item passes quality check, within 2 business days of pickup. ZentroMart Plus members: at pickup.
- **Prepaid order refused at the door or not delivered after 3 attempts:** once the parcel reaches the warehouse, usually 7 to 10 calendar days after the last delivery attempt.

## [REF-2] How long refunds take after initiation
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Timelines are counted in business days from the day the refund is initiated.

| Payment method | Time to reach you |
| --- | --- |
| UPI | 1 to 3 business days |
| Credit or debit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Net banking | 3 to 5 business days |
| ZentroMart Wallet | Instant, within 2 hours |
| Bank transfer (NEFT) for cash-on-delivery orders | 5 to 7 business days |
| ZentroMart Wallet for cash-on-delivery orders | Instant, within 2 hours |

## [REF-3] Cash-on-delivery refunds
For cash-on-delivery orders, the customer chooses either ZentroMart Wallet (instant) or a bank transfer. Bank transfers start only after the bank account details are verified, then take 5 to 7 business days.

## [REF-4] Partial refunds and coupons
If part of an order is returned, the refund is the price paid for that item after its share of any coupon discount. If the remaining order falls below the coupon's minimum order value, the coupon discount on the remaining items is deducted from the refund.

## [REF-5] Delivery and COD fees
Delivery fees and the ₹25 cash-on-delivery fee are refunded only when the whole order is cancelled before shipping or returned because it was damaged, defective or wrong.

## [REF-6] Refund reference number
Every initiated refund has a refund reference number (RRN) that the customer's bank can trace. Share it when a customer says the money has not arrived.
