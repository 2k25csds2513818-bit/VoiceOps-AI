# ZentroMart Plus membership

## [PLS-1] Price
ZentroMart Plus costs ₹999 a year.

## [PLS-2] Benefits
- Free Express delivery where Express is available.
- No reverse pickup fee on any return.
- Refunds on returns are initiated at pickup, without waiting for quality check.

ZentroMart Plus does **not** change return windows, return eligibility or refund timelines after initiation.

## [PLS-3] Cancelling membership
Membership can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund if no benefit has been used.
