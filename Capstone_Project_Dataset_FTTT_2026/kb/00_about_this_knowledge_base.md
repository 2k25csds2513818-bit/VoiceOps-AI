# About this knowledge base

## [KB-00.1] Which document wins
When two documents disagree, follow this order of precedence:

1. **Policy documents** (doc_type: policy) with status `current`.
2. **Agent guides** (doc_type: agent_guide).
3. **Help articles** (doc_type: help).
4. **FAQs** (doc_type: faq).
5. **Marketing pages** (doc_type: marketing). These describe benefits in general terms and never override a policy.


Documents marked `status: superseded` are kept for reference only. They apply only to the cases their successor document says they still cover.

## [KB-00.2] Today's date
All records in this dataset are frozen on **20 October 2026**. Treat this date as "today" when you calculate deadlines, delays and refund timelines.

## [KB-00.3] Business days
A business day is Monday to Saturday, excluding the warehouse holidays below. Refund and logistics timelines are counted in business days. Return windows are counted in calendar days.

| Date | Holiday |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-08-15 | Independence Day |

| 2026-10-19 | Warehouse closure (festival) |
| 2026-11-09 | Warehouse closure (festival) |
