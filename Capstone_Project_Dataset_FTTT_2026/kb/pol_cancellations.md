# Cancellations policy

## [CAN-1] Before the order ships
Any order can be cancelled free of charge until it is shipped, by the customer from My Orders in the app. The support assistant cannot cancel orders itself; it guides the customer to My Orders. The refund is initiated immediately.

## [CAN-2] After the order ships
A shipped order cannot be cancelled. The customer can:
- refuse the parcel at the door. For cash-on-delivery orders nothing is charged. For prepaid orders the refund follows REF-1 once the parcel returns to the warehouse; or
- accept the delivery and return the item within its return window, if the category is returnable.

## [CAN-3] Grocery delivery slots
Grocery orders can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the start of the chosen delivery slot.
If the slot start time is not in the order record, ask the customer for the time shown in My Orders before confirming whether the cutoff has passed.

## [CAN-4] Partial cancellation
Individual items in a multi-item order can be cancelled before they ship. Coupon rules in REF-4 apply.
