# Payments and cash on delivery

## [PAY-1] Payment methods
UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, ZentroMart Wallet and cash on delivery (COD).

## [PAY-2] Cash on delivery
COD is available on orders up to ₹10,000, only on pincodes where COD is enabled. A COD fee of ₹25 applies per order.

## [PAY-3] Failed payments
If money was deducted but the order was not placed, the amount is reversed automatically by the payment provider within 5 business days. No ticket is needed before that.

## [PAY-4] ZentroMart Wallet
ZentroMart Wallet balance can be used on any order and never expires. It cannot be transferred to a bank account. For cash-on-delivery refunds, the customer chooses Wallet or bank transfer before the refund is made (REF-3); once refunded to the Wallet, it stays there.
