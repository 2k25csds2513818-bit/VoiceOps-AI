# Marketplace sellers

## [MKS-1] Who ships your order
Each product in the catalog has a `fulfilled_by` field. Items are either fulfilled by ZentroMart or by an independent marketplace seller.

## [MKS-2] Returns and refunds
Return windows, reverse pickup and refunds are the same for every seller and are handled by ZentroMart.

## [MKS-3] Warranty
ZentroMart Assured never applies to marketplace seller items, even if the listing says assured. Seller items carry only the brand warranty.
