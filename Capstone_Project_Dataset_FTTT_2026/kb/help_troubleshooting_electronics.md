# Troubleshooting common electronics issues

Try these steps before raising a return or warranty claim.

## [TRB-1] Wireless earbuds: one side not working
1. Put both earbuds in the case and close the lid for 10 seconds.
2. Remove the earbuds from the phone's Bluetooth list ("forget device").
3. Hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes, then pair again.

## [TRB-2] Smartwatch not syncing
1. Make sure the companion app has Bluetooth and location permission.
2. Restart the watch and the phone.
3. Unpair the watch in the app and pair it again.

## [TRB-3] Phone charging slowly
1. Use the original cable and adapter.
2. Clean the charging port gently with a dry, soft brush.
3. Turn on airplane mode while charging to check whether background apps are the cause.

## [TRB-4] Bluetooth speaker not powering on
1. Charge it for at least 30 minutes with the original cable.
2. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to force a restart.

If the problem continues after these steps, the item can be returned within its window or claimed under warranty.
