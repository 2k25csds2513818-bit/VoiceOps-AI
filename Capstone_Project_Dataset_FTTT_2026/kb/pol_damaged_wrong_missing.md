# Damaged, wrong or missing items

## [DWM-1] Reporting window
Report a damaged, defective, expired, wrong or missing item within **48 hours of delivery**, with photos of the item and the packaging. This applies to every category, including grocery and other non-returnable items.

## [DWM-2] Resolution
After verification the customer gets a free replacement or a full refund, including delivery fees. No reverse pickup fee applies.

## [DWM-3] High-value items
Claims for items worth more than ₹10,000 are handled by the Priority Resolution team. The support agent must create a ticket and hand over; the agent cannot approve these claims.

## [DWM-4] Reported after 48 hours
Reports after 48 hours are handled as a normal return if the item is still within its return window. Otherwise the customer can use the brand or ZentroMart Assured warranty, if any.

## [DWM-5] Safety issues
If a product shows a safety risk, such as a swelling battery, burning smell, sparks or heat, the customer must stop using it immediately. The agent must hand over to a human at once.
