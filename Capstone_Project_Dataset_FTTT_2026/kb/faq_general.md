# Frequently asked questions

## [FAQ-1] How long do refunds take?
Refunds usually take 5 to 7 days to reach your account.

## [FAQ-2] Can I return something I bought?
Most items can be returned within the return window. Some items, like grocery, cannot be returned.

## [FAQ-3] Is delivery free?
Delivery is free on most orders.

## [FAQ-4] Can I talk to a person?
Yes. Ask the assistant to connect you to a human colleague.

## [FAQ-5] Do you deliver outside India?
We currently deliver only within India.
