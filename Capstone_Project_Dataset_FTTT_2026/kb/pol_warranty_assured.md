# Warranty and ZentroMart Assured

## [WAR-1] Brand warranty
Most electronics come with the manufacturer's warranty. The warranty period is listed in the `warranty_months` column of the product catalog. Brand warranty claims are made with the manufacturer's service centre.

## [WAR-2] ZentroMart Assured
Products marked `assured = true` in the catalog carry a 12-month ZentroMart Assured warranty from the delivery date, in addition to any brand warranty. Claims are raised in the app under My Orders, and ZentroMart arranges pickup and repair or replacement.

## [WAR-3] What warranty does not cover
Physical damage, liquid damage, normal wear and unauthorised repairs are not covered.
