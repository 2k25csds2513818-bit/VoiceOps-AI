# Reverse pickup: how it works

## [RVP-1] Booking
Raise the return from My Orders. Choose a pickup slot. The delivery executive will check the item at the door against the condition rules in RET-4.

## [RVP-2] Missed pickup
If the executive does not arrive, the pickup is rescheduled automatically to the next business day. After two missed pickups, the customer can ask support to raise a priority pickup.

## [RVP-3] Item rejected at the door
If the item fails the doorstep check, the executive will not collect it, and the customer gets an SMS with the reason.
