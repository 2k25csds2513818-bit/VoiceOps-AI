# Delivery and shipping policy

## [DEL-1] Delivery times
| Delivery type | Metro cities | Other locations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3 to 6 business days | 5 to 9 business days |
| Express (selected pincodes) | Next business day if ordered before 2 pm, otherwise the business day after | Not available |

Each order shows a promised delivery date at checkout. That date is the commitment.

## [DEL-2] Delivery fees
Standard delivery is free on orders of ₹499 or more; below that a ₹40 fee applies. Express delivery costs ₹79 and is free for ZentroMart Plus members.

## [DEL-3] Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After a failed attempt the customer gets an SMS to reschedule. If the third attempt fails, the parcel is returned to the warehouse (return to origin, RTO) and REF-1 applies.

## [DEL-4] Late deliveries
If an **Express** order arrives after its promised date, the ₹79 express fee the customer paid is credited to ZentroMart Wallet automatically within 48 hours. ZentroMart Plus members, who pay no express fee, get no credit. There is no compensation for late Standard deliveries. Agents must not offer any other compensation.

## [DEL-5] Where we deliver
ZentroMart delivers only within India, to pincodes listed as serviceable in the pincode table. Cash on delivery and Express are available only where the pincode table says so (`cod_available`, `express_available`). Not every metro pincode has Express.

## [DEL-6] Changing the address
The delivery address can be changed before the order ships. After shipping, the address cannot be changed.
