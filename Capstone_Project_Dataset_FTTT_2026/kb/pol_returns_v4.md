# Returns policy (version 4)

## [RET-1] Scope
This policy covers items bought on ZentroMart, whether shipped by ZentroMart or by a marketplace seller. Seller-specific warranty terms are in the Marketplace sellers policy, but return windows are the same for every seller.

## [RET-2] Return windows by category
The return window starts on the **delivery date** and is counted in calendar days. The category of every product is listed in the `return_category` column of the product catalog.

| Category | Return window | Catalog code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electronics and accessories | 7 days | `electronics` |
| Clothing, footwear and bags | 15 days | `fashion` |
| Home, kitchen and furniture | 10 days | `home_kitchen` |
| Beauty and personal care (unopened, seal intact) | 7 days | `beauty_sealed` |
| Books and stationery | 10 days | `books_media` |
| Grocery, fresh and perishables | Not returnable | `grocery` |
| Innerwear, lingerie and swimwear | Not returnable | `innerwear` |
| Gift cards and digital codes | Not returnable | `digital` |

## [RET-3] Change to electronics returns
From **1 August 2026**, the return window for electronics and accessories is 7 days. Electronics **delivered before 1 August 2026** keep the earlier 10-day window from version 3 of this policy.

## [RET-4] Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, with original tags, packaging, accessories and free gifts. Beauty and personal care products are returnable only if unopened with the seal intact. Electronics must not be physically damaged, and any screen lock or account lock must be removed.

## [RET-5] Items that cannot be returned
Grocery and fresh produce, innerwear, lingerie and swimwear, and gift cards or digital codes cannot be returned for change of mind. If such an item arrives damaged, expired, wrong or missing, follow the Damaged, wrong or missing items policy.

## [RET-6] Reverse pickup and fees
Returns are collected from the delivery address through reverse pickup. A reverse pickup fee of ₹49 applies to change-of-mind returns of clothing, footwear and bags. The fee is waived for ZentroMart Plus members and for any item returned because it was damaged, defective or wrong.

## [RET-7] After pickup
Returned items go through a quality check (QC) at the warehouse. The refund is initiated within 2 business days of pickup once the item passes QC. For ZentroMart Plus members, the refund is initiated at the time of pickup. Refund timelines after initiation are in the Refunds policy.

## [RET-8] Festive sale returns
Clothing, footwear and bags from orders **placed between 01 October and 15 October 2026** can be returned within 30 days of delivery. All other categories follow the normal windows in RET-2.

## [RET-9] Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or colour are available on clothing and footwear within the return window, subject to stock. If the new size is out of stock, a return and refund is offered instead.
